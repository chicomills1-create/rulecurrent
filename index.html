Accuracy is the product

Source-linked,
or it doesn't ship.

Every RuleCurrent item is checked against the live official City of Phoenix source at publish time — never written from a scrape, a screenshot, a third-party summary, or memory. If we cannot establish a change from the city's own publication, the item does not go out.

FORMAT

Every item, same five parts

What changed · the official source (direct link) · the source's own effective or posting date · who it affects · the action checklist — what your firm does differently on its next filing.

CORRECTIONS

Corrected visibly, always

If an item is ever wrong or goes stale, the correction goes to every subscriber, marked as a correction, the record is amended, and the incident is logged. That policy is part of what you are buying.

BOUNDARIES

Information, never authority

RuleCurrent is not affiliated with the City of Phoenix or any agency. We never guarantee an approval, a timeline, or an outcome, and nothing we publish is legal advice or engineering judgment. The city's documents govern — we point at them, accurately and fast.

Sample Pulse item — real, verified November 1, 2026

Electrical / HVAC — NEC GFCI enforcement deferred to March 1, 2027

What changed: The City of Phoenix is deferring enforcement of the 2023 NEC 210.8(F) Exception 2 GFCI requirement for HVAC equipment, to ensure adequate product availability. Full enforcement will now take effect March 1, 2027.
Official source: City of Phoenix — Building Construction Codes · Source posting date: not stated on the source page · RuleCurrent verification date: November 1, 2026
Who it affects: Electrical and HVAC filers on Phoenix projects with outdoor HVAC equipment.

  • Carry March 1, 2027 as the enforcement date in project standards — not the original date
  • Confirm equipment GFCI-protection availability per project before specifying
  • Open the source page and confirm the notice is still posted before relying on this item
